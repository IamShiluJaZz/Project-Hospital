About Rivershore Hospital

Our Commitment to Healthcare Excellence

Rivershore Hospital is a premier healthcare institution dedicated to providing high-quality medical services with a patient-centered approach. Located in Poonoor, Kozhikode, we integrate advanced technology with expert care to enhance hospital management and patient experience.

Hospital Overview

Rivershore Hospital, situated in Poonoor, Kozhikode, is home to a team of 22 dedicated doctors, supported by 19 trainees and 110 skilled staff members. We are committed to delivering exceptional medical care through well-equipped facilities and a compassionate approach, ensuring that every patient receives the best possible treatment. Our hospital is designed to provide a seamless healthcare experience, combining advanced medical technology with personalized care. With a strong focus on patient safety, comfort, and well-being, we continuously strive to enhance our services and uphold the highest standards of medical excellence.
